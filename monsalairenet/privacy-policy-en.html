Privacy Policy

Mon Salaire Net Application

Important: Mon Salaire Net respects your privacy. No personally identifiable data (name, email, contact information) is collected by this application.

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the "Mon Salaire Net" application collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information Collected

2.1 Personal Data

Mon Salaire Net does NOT collect ANY personally identifiable data. We do not collect:

  • First or last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Bank account information
  • Any other information that allows personal identification

2.2 Calculation Data

The salary amounts you enter for calculations are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or third parties. This data remains private and under your control.

2.3 Technical and Analytics Data

To improve the quality and stability of the application, we use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. These services collect anonymous technical information such as:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Diagnostic data in case of crashes
  • Anonymous usage statistics (pages viewed, features used)

This data is collected anonymously and does not allow personal identification.

3. Use of the URSSAF API

Mon Salaire Net uses the public URSSAF API to perform social security contribution and net salary calculations. This API is used to:

  • Obtain current contribution rates
  • Calculate social security contribution amounts
  • Convert gross salary to net salary

Requests sent to the URSSAF API do not contain any personal information about you. Only anonymous calculation data (amounts, applicable rates) are exchanged.

4. Data Usage

Technical data collected via Firebase is used solely to:

  • Detect and fix bugs and application crashes
  • Understand how the application is used to improve user experience
  • Optimize application performance

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. The only data shared is anonymous technical data collected by Firebase, which is hosted on Google's servers in accordance with their privacy policy.

6. Data Security

We take security seriously. Calculation data remains local on your device. Technical data collected is transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Third-Party Services

This application uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics (Google): For technical tracking and application improvement. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • URSSAF API: For social security contribution calculations. URSSAF Privacy Policy

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personally identifiable data, there is no personal data to view, modify, or delete. You maintain full control over calculation data that remains on your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. In case of significant changes, we will notify you via an in-app notification or an update to this page. We encourage you to review this policy regularly.

10. Contact

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or the use of your data, you can contact us by email: serabile@gmail.com
